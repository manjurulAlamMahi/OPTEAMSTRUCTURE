1

Project Assignment

Who hands the project to whom, from the sheet down to the developer.

  1. 07 KAM Sheet

    New project logged

    Every new project starts here. It's the single source for all incoming work.

  2. PC · Project Coordinator

    Pick up & assign

    Takes the project from the sheet, checks deadline & scope, sets priority and assigns it to an Operations Manager.

  3. OM · Operations Manager

    Choose the PM

    Assigns the project to the Project Manager whose teams match the stack.

  4. PM · Project Manager

    Plan & hand out tasks

    Breaks the project into tasks and assigns them to developers.

  5. DEV · Developer

    Build

    Completes the tasks, updates GitHub and sends progress updates back to the PM.

Project Handling & Tracking

How a project is prioritised, routed to the right team and kept on schedule until delivery.

PC · Project Coordinator

Check deadline & set priority

Reviews deadline, scope and urgency, then passes the project to the OM with a clear priority.

OM · Operations Manager

Decide who builds it

Checks priority and required skills.

SPT · Special / Rapid Team

Special Team builds it

OM assigns the project to the Special Team and names a PM to track it. The Special Team focuses only on the work.

PM · Project Manager

PM tracks & reports

Follows progress, runs client meetings and passes every update between the Special Team and Sales.

No
PM · Project Manager

PM's team builds it

OM assigns it to a PM. The PM assigns tasks to developers, runs meetings and makes sure work is completed.

PM · Project Manager

PM tracks & reports

Tracks tasks, holds client meetings and keeps Sales updated.

PM → Sales

Delivery & handover

PM confirms the work is complete and reports delivery to Sales. Delivery & cancel data flows back up to OM, PC and management.

PC · Project Coordinator

Deadline watch

From assignment to delivery, the PC keeps checking deadlines on the Project Tracker and flags risks to the OM early.

Continuous
  • One voice to Sales. The Special Team never talks to Sales directly. All updates go through the assigned PM.
  • Every project has a PM. Even when the Special Team builds it, a PM owns tracking, meetings and reporting.
  • Priority is set once, at the top. The PC sets it and the OM uses it to decide Special Team vs. regular team.
